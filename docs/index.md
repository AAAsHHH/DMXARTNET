# DMXnetART Documentation

This documentation covers the **Wi‑Fi Art‑Net → DMX converter** (DMXnetART v1.2): a compact device that receives Art‑Net lighting data over Wi‑Fi and outputs standard DMX512 to lighting fixtures.

## What is this device?

DMXnetART is a bridge between:

- PC or controller software that speaks **Art‑Net** over Ethernet/Wi‑Fi  
- Conventional **DMX512** lighting fixtures (moving heads, LED pars, dimmers, etc.)

The converter:

- Connects to your Wi‑Fi network  
- Subscribes to 1–3 Art‑Net universes  
- Outputs the corresponding DMX channels on a standard DMX connector (XLR)

## Who is this for?

- Stage and DJ lighting setups  
- Architectural and installation lighting  
- Small to medium LED shows  
- PC-based control (QLC+, Lightkey, Resolume, TouchDesigner, custom software, etc.)
